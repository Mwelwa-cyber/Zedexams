Why we need somethingYour age decides which lessons you get and whether we need to ask a grown-up first. We'd rather ask than guess.
Never a dead end. Some children genuinely don't know their date of birth, and a required three-field form simply stops them. Year-only or grade-derived is enough to age-band correctly — and when a rough age lands near a boundary, treat it as under 18 and ask a guardian. Fail safe, not fail open.

While you waitYou can start learning straight away — lessons and past papers are ready. A few things switch on once your grown-up says yes.
Not a rejection. Under-18 flows straight into the guardian step as a normal next page — no "sorry, you're too young", no back button that invites a second guess. A child who learns that a different answer unlocks more will simply give a different answer, which defeats the whole gate.
