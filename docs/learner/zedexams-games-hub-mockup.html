Games hub — proposed fix

Same content, same brand, same backend. What changes is density, overflow behaviour and the fixed chrome: nothing gets covered by the bottom nav or the Ask Zed pill, no text wraps mid-phrase, and every card is one predictable height.

What changed, and why

Ordered worst-first. Items marked BUG are broken on the live page right now, not taste.

1
Nothing sits under the bottom nav any more bug Live, the nav is translucent and the page has no bottom padding, so “Your games”, the Leaderboard link and the first game card render through it. Nav is now opaque with a top border, and the scroll body ends in padding-bottom: calc(60px + 62px + safe-area) — nav height, plus the Ask Zed pill, plus the notch inset.
2
Ask Zed stops covering content bug It currently lands on the “Centurion” badge label, on Punctuation Pro and on Map Quest. It now docks above the nav (bottom: nav + 14px), is shorter (44px), hides on scroll-down and returns on scroll-up (reuse useHideOnScroll, already in the codebase), and the reserved bottom padding means the last card can always be scrolled clear of it.
3
The daily quiz is serving the wrong grade bug The hero says “Grade 3” while the challenge card beside it says “Grade 7”. Same bug as the audit found (two code paths, one not grade-filtered). The grade now renders as a pill on both heroes, from one source, so a mismatch is visible instantly instead of hiding in an eyebrow.
4
No more mid-phrase wrapping “TODAY'S QUIZ · GRADE / 3”, “Race another / learner!”, “Fractions, Decimals & / Percent” all break across lines today because the type is sized for a tablet. Titles are 17.5px, meta 12px, and every one-line string is nowrap + ellipsis, so a long game name truncates cleanly instead of reflowing the card.
5
Empty progress bars are gone honesty Three of five games show a 0%-filled bar next to “Not played yet” — a bar that measures nothing. And a “best score” isn’t a completion percentage, so Word Builder’s bar reading 100% at “Best 120” is meaningless. Status is now one pill: Play, Best 120, or New.
6
Every game card is the same height Two wrapping chips made Meaning Match ~40px taller than Punctuation Pro. Chips become a single truncating meta line, subject · topic. Cards are 68px, so five fit in one viewport instead of two and a half.
7
The header block earns its space “Games / Play, score, level up!” plus a floating back button costs ~110px to say nothing — and Games is a bottom-nav tab, so a back chevron is the wrong affordance. Replaced by a 52px bar: title, streak chip, leaderboard. That’s the whole first hero card recovered.
8
Achievements scroll cleanly The tan native scrollbar under the row is now hidden, with scroll-snap and a visible 5th tile edge to signal there’s more. Locked badges render grey with a padlock so “9 / 10” is legible on the shelf itself.
9
Child-readable copy copy “same questions, server keeps score” is engineering reassurance written for you, not for an 11-year-old — and it pushed the card to three lines. Now just “5 quick questions”. Fairness is the promise; the server is the implementation.
10
The level card is a strip, not a billboard It was 150px tall for one number, with a decorative circle bleeding over the right edge and under the text. Now 78px, with “224 XP to Level 7” next to the level rather than below it, and the decoration clipped inside the card.
11
Worth a look: Meaning Match content It’s named and iconed like a vocabulary game (🧠, “meaning”) but tagged Mathematics · Fractions, Decimals & Percent. Either the name or the mapping is wrong — a child hunting for a maths game won’t find it there.
