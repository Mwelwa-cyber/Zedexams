Waiting is not blocking. The child goes straight into lessons and past papers. Naming what's on now and what's still off does two useful things: it stops the wait feeling like a punishment, and it gives the child a real reason to go and nudge their guardian. Resend and change the number must both be here — this is where a wrong number gets caught.
