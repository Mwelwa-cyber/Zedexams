Two rungs added to the requested list, and each is load-bearing

Multiplying has to sit before dividing, because dividing fractions is multiplying by the flipped one — teaching division first teaches a rule with nothing under it. Fractions, decimals & percentages exists because ECZ papers move between the three constantly, and a learner who has only met fractions freezes when the same amount turns up as 0.5 or 50%.

And one rung earlier than you would think

Equal fractions has to come before adding. You cannot add 12 + 14 without first knowing 12 = 24. Skip it and level 4 stops being a step and becomes a wall. Un-pass level 2 in the rig and watch level 4 shut.

No level is skippable, and the reason is still declared

A level opens when the level before it is passed and every level it names in prereq[] is. The sequence is what stops a learner jumping ahead — un-pass a middle level in the rig and everything above it shuts, in order. The declared list is what lets a lock say why rather than only "it is next": level 4 cites equal fractions, level 7 cites multiplying, level 9 cites all four operations. Both halves are checked below, including one that passes every other level and confirms each one still refuses to open without its own predecessor.

Stars never gate anything

A level opens when its prerequisites are passed. Three stars are a reason to feel good, not a toll — a child who scrapes a pass still moves on, and a locked level always names the level that unlocks it rather than showing a padlock and nothing else.

Each level splits into ordered sub-steps

"Adding fractions" is not one skill — it is five, and the order is the teaching: same bottom, one bottom fits the other, no common bottom, answers over one, then a word problem. Smallest jump first, one new difficulty per sub-step. Sequencing this is the difference between a child climbing and a child stuck.

Level 9 is the capstone, not the introduction

Word problems ask a learner to do two things at once — decide which operation, then perform it. It goes last, once the operations are automatic, and it is the only level whose prerequisites are all four operations plus level 8. Kwacha, mealie meal, chitenge and time, so the story is one they recognise.

Every stage mixes new work with the learner's own misses

composeStage() is a pure function of (level, sub-step, the learner's tricky pool, stage number, learner salt). It draws review items that are DUE and never lets them take more than half a stage — so no two children walk the same path, and switching learner in the rig genuinely reshuffles both halves.

The level definition is data, not code

level { id, order, name, blurb, exampleExpr, prereq[], subSteps[] } and subStep { order, name, hint, questionBand, stageRange }. Nothing in the engine below is fraction-specific — decimals, ratio and geometry get their own file of the same shape.
